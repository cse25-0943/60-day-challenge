# 🍎 NutriScope – Personal Nutrition Intelligence Dashboard

NutriScope is a modern, multi-page nutrition analysis web application designed to help users understand their dietary habits through intelligent nutrient tracking, meal planning, and personalized recommendations.

Built using **HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Chart.js, and LocalStorage**, NutriScope provides a complete nutrition monitoring experience without requiring any backend services.

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Advanced Dashboard

* Daily calorie tracking and energy balance
* Interactive macro distribution charts
* Nutrient score analysis
* Top nutrient deficiencies and excesses
* Enhanced micronutrient status monitoring

### 🍽️ Smart Food Logging

* Add, edit, and remove food entries
* Support for over **60 common foods**
* Quantity and unit customization
* Meal categorization (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks)
* CSV upload functionality for bulk food imports

### 📅 2-Day Meal Planner

* Personalized meal suggestions
* Day-wise meal scheduling
* Calorie-balanced recommendations
* Flexible meal customization

### 🧬 Comprehensive Nutrient Tracking

Track essential nutrients including:

* Calories
* Protein
* Carbohydrates
* Fat
* Fiber
* Iron
* Calcium
* Vitamin C
* Vitamin D
* Vitamin B12
* Zinc
* Magnesium
* Potassium
* Sodium
* Selenium
* Vitamin A
* Vitamin K
* Folate
* Omega-3 Fatty Acids

### 🩺 Risk Analysis Engine

NutriScope evaluates potential nutritional risks based on intake patterns:

* Nutrient Deficiency Risk
* Bone Health Risk
* Heart Health Risk
* Diabetes Risk Indicators
* Overall Nutrition Risk Assessment

### 🤖 Intelligent Recommendations

Receive evidence-based recommendations including:

* Food additions for deficiencies
* Healthier food swaps
* Portion adjustment suggestions
* Diet-specific guidance for:

  * Vegetarian
  * Eggetarian
  * Non-Vegetarian users

### 📚 Educational Support

* Nutrition educational disclaimers
* Trusted nutrition information sources
* Awareness about healthy eating habits
* Encouragement to seek professional healthcare advice when necessary
