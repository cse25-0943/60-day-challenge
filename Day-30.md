## 📖 About the Project

Supply Chain Builder is an educational simulation that helps beginners understand how real-world supply chains work through interactive decision-making.

Instead of reading theory, users build a complete supply chain for a randomly generated company and instantly see how each business decision affects cost, speed, risk, sustainability, and customer satisfaction.

The project was designed with a modern enterprise dashboard UI inspired by professional logistics and business management software.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎲 Random Company Generation
Every playthrough creates a different company with:

- Industry
- Products
- Countries Served
- Demand Level

This makes every simulation unique.

---

### 🏗 Build Your Supply Chain

Players make strategic decisions for:

- 📦 Supplier Strategy
  - Single Supplier
  - Multiple Suppliers

- 🏭 Factory Location

- 🏬 Warehouse Strategy

- 🚚 Transportation Method
  - Road
  - Rail
  - Sea
  - Air

- 📦 Inventory Strategy
  - Low Inventory
  - Balanced Inventory
  - High Inventory

---

### 📊 Live Business Dashboard

Every decision updates business KPIs in real time.

Metrics include:

- 💰 Cost
- ⚡ Delivery Speed
- ⚠ Risk
- 😊 Customer Satisfaction
- 🌱 Sustainability

---

### 🎯 Final Supply Chain Score

After completing all decisions, the simulator generates:

- Overall Supply Chain Score (0–100)
- Performance Rating
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Biggest Business Risk
- Three Practical Improvement Suggestions
- Decision Recap

## 🎮 How It Works

1. Start the simulation.
2. A random company is generated.
3. Learn each supply chain concept through beginner-friendly explanations.
4. Make business decisions.
5. Watch live metrics change.
6. Receive your Supply Chain Score and recommendations.
