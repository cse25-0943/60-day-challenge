### ⚽ Football Intelligence Hub

A multi-stage AI-powered football experience that combines tournament analysis, football knowledge assessment, and personality profiling into one interactive report.

This project transforms football data into personalized insights, helping users explore their football awareness, predict major tournament outcomes, and discover whether their personality aligns more closely with legendary players like Messi or Ronaldo.



## 🚀 Features

### 🌍 FIFA World Cup 2026 Prediction Report

Analyze historical trends, team strength, player performance, and tournament patterns to generate:

* 🏆 Predicted Winner
* 🥈 Predicted Runner-Up
* 🐎 Dark Horse Nation
* ⭐ Players to Watch
* 📊 Confidence Scores (0–100%)
* ⚠️ Risks & Counterarguments
* 📈 Evidence-Based Analysis


### 🧠 Football IQ Quiz

An adaptive football knowledge assessment featuring:

* Beginner to Advanced questions
* Multiple-choice format
* Automatic scoring system
* Football Awareness Score (0–100)
* Personalized fan classification

Classifications include:

* Beginner Fan
* Casual Viewer
* Football Follower
* Football Enthusiast
* Football Expert

The report highlights:

* Strongest knowledge areas
* Weakest areas
* Knowledge gaps
* Improvement recommendations



### 🐐 Messi vs Ronaldo Personality Match

A personality assessment that evaluates:

* Ambition
* Discipline
* Leadership
* Teamwork
* Creativity
* Competitiveness
* Confidence
* Work Ethic
* Learning Style
* Decision-Making Style

Without directly asking "Messi or Ronaldo?" questions, the system calculates:

* 🔵 Ronaldo Compatibility %
* 🔴 Messi Compatibility %
* Detailed reasoning for both matches
* Personality-based football insights



### 🎯 Football Personality Archetype

Receive one personalized archetype:

* Creative Playmaker
* Relentless Competitor
* Tactical Visionary
* Quiet Leader
* Fearless Attacker
* Strategic Commander
* Consistent Performer
* Big-Match Specialist

Each archetype includes:

* Key strengths
* Playing mentality
* Leadership style
* Decision-making tendencies



### 📋 Final Football Intelligence Profile

A consolidated report containing:

✅ World Cup 2026 Predictions

✅ Football Awareness Score

✅ Fan Classification

✅ Messi Compatibility

✅ Ronaldo Compatibility

✅ Football Personality Archetype

✅ Recommended Players

✅ Recommended Clubs

✅ Recommended National Teams

✅ Recommended Rivalries

✅ Personalized Insights Summary
