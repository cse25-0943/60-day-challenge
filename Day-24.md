# 🚀 LocalPro – Business Strategy & Sustainability Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

**LocalPro** is a verified local services marketplace designed for **Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities in India**. The platform connects customers with trusted service providers such as electricians, plumbers, tutors, cleaners, and repair technicians through a simple, low-cost matching system powered by **WhatsApp**.

This project presents the complete **Business Strategy & Sustainability Dashboard**, analyzing the startup's business model, revenue strategy, GTM roadmap, risks, competitive positioning, and investment readiness.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Finding trustworthy local service providers remains a challenge in many Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities. Existing platforms often struggle with:

* ❌ Lack of trust
* ❌ High customer acquisition costs
* ❌ Low provider retention
* ❌ Poor service quality
* ❌ Weak local network effects

LocalPro aims to solve these issues with a lightweight, validation-first marketplace.
